## ATLAS 1.3.0

**A friendlier installer, a conversation you can just keep having, and every setting by voice.**

- **New installer window - no more console questions.** Windows and macOS now open a small setup window: a Welcome screen, an **Options** page (a checkbox per optional item with a one-line explanation and its download size - Hermes, Ollama, speech models, the wake-word model, CUDA only if you have an NVIDIA GPU, desktop shortcut, start at sign-in), then a **step list with check marks**, a status line and a progress bar. Hermes and Ollama are installed from inside the window. Press **Finish** and ATLAS opens straight into its **AI sign-in** (ChatGPT / Claude / Grok / OpenRouter / Local), then asks your **name, city and favorite sports**. Updates run silently and reuse your earlier choices.
- **Conversation mode.** After ATLAS speaks an answer (push-to-talk or "Hey ATLAS") it listens again for a few seconds - 6 s by default, 3-15 s in Settings, on by default - so you can reply without pressing anything. A **countdown ring** and a **soft tone** show the window; it stops quietly if you say nothing, and "that's all", "stop", "thanks", Esc or the mic button end it. It starts only after the audio has finished, so ATLAS never hears itself.
- **Change any setting by voice or chat.** "Turn off wake word", "mute", "turn off conversation mode", "set the listening window to 10 seconds", "change your name to Nova", "set my city to Dallas", "hide the weather panel", "switch to Claude", "turn off automatic updates", "what are my settings" - answered locally with a spoken confirmation. Risky ones (microphone on, add-on modules, switching the AI, removing a sport, reset, uninstall, installing an update) ask you to say yes first. A new **General** tab in Settings shows the same switches.
- **Remove sports and teams.** In Settings -> Profile -> Sports every team/league chip has an X (saved at once, Sports tile updates immediately), you can edit a team, and removing a whole sport asks first. By voice: "remove soccer", "stop following Juventus".
- **Fixed: blank white-paper desktop icon.** The shortcut now points at the real icon (a multi-size .ico, 16-256 px) and the icon cache is refreshed.
- **Includes everything from 1.2.1:** the "Assignment to constant variable" wake-word fix, weak local model handling (hermes3 and other small models no longer answer with example tool-call text; recommended models; **Test my AI**), and long answers are no longer cut at 300 characters.
- Updating from 1.2.x is automatic (Settings -> Updates). Your data and settings are kept.
